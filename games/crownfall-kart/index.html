    01 / TAKE THE LINE

    W or ↑ accelerates. A/D or ←/→ steer. S or ↓ brakes and reverses. Light karts turn tighter; heavy karts hit harder. Touch players can enable auto accelerate.

    02 / BEND THE RULES

    Turn and press Space to hop. Hold it through the corner. Blue, orange and purple sparks charge three turbo levels. Release to launch. Tap Space just before landing from a ramp for a trick boost.

    03 / CLAIM THE CHAOS

    Ride cyan boost pads, jump broken bridges, and draft close behind rivals for a slipstream boost. Collect glowing rift boxes. Press E or tap your item to use it. Leaders get defenses; stragglers get miracles. Boosts cut through mud. R returns you to your last checkpoint.

    04 / MORE WAYS TO FALL

    Rift Sprint: a short start-to-finish run. Grand Prix: four races, most points wins. Time trial: three clean laps and a saved personal-best ghost. Battle: pop your rivals’ three balloons; last survivor wins. Gamepad: stick, R2 drive, L2 brake, A drift, X item.
