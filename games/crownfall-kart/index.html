    How to play

    Driving basics

    Drive

    W or ↑ accelerates. A/D or ←/→ steer. S or ↓ brakes and reverses. Light karts turn tighter; heavy karts hit harder. Touch players can enable auto accelerate. White arrows show the race direction; amber boards mark bends and jump lines. Red marks a broken edge; cyan marks the landing.

    Drift and turbo

    Turn and press Space to hop. Hold it through the corner. Blue, orange and purple sparks charge three turbo levels. Release for a stronger turbo at each charge tier. Drifting carries more speed through a bend than ordinary full-lock steering. Hop over a floor mine or across mud to keep your momentum. Tap jump at the amber takeoff line for a faster launch and a takeoff boost; tap again just before landing for the strongest jump combo.

    Boosts and items

    Ride cyan boost pads, jump broken bridges, and draft close behind rivals for a slipstream boost. Collect glowing rift boxes. Press E or tap your item to use it. Leaders get defenses; stragglers get miracles. Boosts cut through mud. R returns you to your last checkpoint.

    Modes and controls

    Rift Sprint: a short start-to-finish run. Grand Prix: four races, most points wins. Time trial: three clean laps and a saved personal-best ghost. Battle: pop your rivals’ three balloons; last survivor wins. Gamepad: stick, R2 drive, L2 brake, A drift, X item, Y view. You drive from the cockpit; press C (or tap View) to switch to the hood camera. Spins, falls, ramp jumps and the finish cut to a third-person shot.
