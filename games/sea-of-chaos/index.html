How to plunder

1 · Grab treasure

Glowing chests and gold float on the sea. Sail through them to haul them aboard. The pile on your deck shows what you carry.

2 · Bank it

Sail into your harbor's ring of buoys to bank it. The gold arrow points home. If you sink first, all of it spills into the sea.

3 · Fight

Your cannons fire sideways. Bring a rival alongside and fire; the guns aim themselves. Sinking a ship spills its gold for anyone to grab.

  • Treasure galleon sails across at 2:10 and again at 1:02. Sink it for a hoard of chests.
  • Whirlpools roam the sea and change course without warning. They suck you in and sink you if you reach the eye. Full sail escapes the edge; from the middle only a boost will save you. Spilled treasure circles inside them as bait.
  • Barrels give power-ups: ✹ Big Guns, ➤ Fair Wind, ✚ Repair.
  • Last 30 seconds: banked gold counts double.
  • Your harbor heals you, and its tower shoots raiders.
  • When time runs out, the crew with the most banked gold wins.
