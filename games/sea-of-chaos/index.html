How to Play

Win

  • Be the last ship afloat.
  • If the storm closes first, the highest Match Score wins: sink a ship +500, secure a chest +200, slay a Megalodon, Kraken or Skeleton Ship +350.
  • Stay inside the storm ring. Outside it your hull rots and your crew takes damage.

Crew of two

  • One sails: wheel at the stern, sails at the mast (catch the wind on the compass).
  • One fights and repairs: cannons on each side, harpoon at the bow, patching the hold below the hatch.
  • Alone on a ship? An AI Deckhand joins to patch, bail and trim sails.

Keep her afloat

  • Hits punch breaches in the hold. Water pours in. At 100% you sink.
  • Go down the hatch, hold F on a breach to plank it.
  • Hold F in the hold to fill your bucket, climb up and Click to throw it overboard (or onto a fire).

Stations

  • Wheel: A/D steer.
  • Sails: W/S raise or lower, A/D angle them to the wind.
  • Anchor: F drops it for a handbrake turn; hold F to hoist it back.
  • Cannon / Harpoon: aim with the mouse, Click to fire. Hold Click on the harpoon to reel in.

Cursed arsenal

  • Grog ball: enemy crew goes dizzy.
  • Rigging ball: drops their sails.
  • Anchor ball: drops their anchor.
  • Powder kegs: grab one from the hold crate, carry it aboard an enemy, click to light. Floating kegs are mines.
  • Harpoon: snag ships to board, reel in treasure, or yank pirates off their deck.
