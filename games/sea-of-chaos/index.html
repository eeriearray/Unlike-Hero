WELCOME TO DOCK WARS

Build. Defend.
Break through.

  1. 1 · GEAR UP · FIRST 25 SECONDS Both forts start shielded. Follow the gold arrow to treasure, then sail home to bank it. Your ship upgrades itself at the dock (switch to manual in the Dock shop).
  2. 2 · RAID THEIR FORT Shoot their shield until it pops, then sink their two turrets. Turrets fire back, and your home water repairs you.
  3. 3 · BOARD THE CORE · 3 SECONDS Sail slowly into the glowing ring by their core. Each hit you take knocks a second off. A destroyed core means no more respawns for that crew.
  4. 4 · SINK THE FLEET Sink every ship left on that crew to win the round. First crew to win two rounds wins the match.

If nobody breaks through, the center beacon opens at 3:00: hold its ring alone for 12 seconds to win the round. At 3:45 the storm closes in and respawns stop.

FIRING IS AUTOMATIC

Click or press F to fire. Your cannons shoot from whichever side has a rival nearby. Hold to keep firing. Q and E force the left or right side. Shift boosts, Space rams, 1/2/3 change your shot.

Touch: the left stick sails, the big Fire button shoots.
