Narrator Studio

Record the storyteller once and every game uses your voice. There are short lines. None of them contain player names, so one recording covers every game — the names appear on screen.

Deepen voice

How to sound like a noir narrator

  • Use a quiet room. Hold the phone or mic a hand's width from your mouth.
  • Speak lower and slower than normal, like you're telling a ghost story. Lean into the pauses at "..."
  • Press Record (or the R key), wait for the click, read the line, press it again to stop. Silence at the start and end is trimmed automatically.
  • Each take is saved straight into games/mafia/voice/. Commit that folder and every player hears you.
  • Prefer an AI voice? Generate each line in a service like ElevenLabs and use Upload on its row.
