PARTY
PACK

Six party games. Your phones are the controllers.

Main StageOpen this on the TV or computer everyone can see. Join on a phoneEnter the 4-character room code from the big screen. AudienceWatch, vote and fill the Hype Meter. Prompt StudioWrite your own prompt packs and share them with a 7-digit code. Voice BoothRecord the hosts' lines in a real voice. Open it on the Main Stage computer.

Playing on several devices? Run npm start in the Unlike Hero folder and open the address it prints on every device. Without it, tabs in the same browser still work.
