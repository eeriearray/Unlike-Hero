What a century does

  • Dry things last. A plank across cracks is still a bridge.
  • Wet things don’t. Leaks and running water rot wood and rust iron.
  • Sunlit soil turns an acorn into an ironwood that leans one way.
  • The Stash Box keeps the century out. The Echo can take it out new.
  • Porous stone carries damp along its connected marked strip.
  • Sediment builds a bank in marked troughs and buries low passages.
  • Iron shores in brass sockets support their slabs only while dry.
