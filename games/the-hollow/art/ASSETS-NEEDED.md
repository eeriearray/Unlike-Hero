# The Hollow — targeted art brief

The installed backgrounds, 512px Cursed Relics illustrations, UI assets and effects are sufficient for the current cinematic presentation. This pass uses those assets in scenes, travel previews and class insignia. More unrelated packs would increase visual inconsistency.

The remaining gaps need artwork from one artist or one coherent pack:

1. **Silent Square exterior:** an abandoned old European village square at night, with a central stone well, notice board on the left, a modest camp/lantern area on the right, and a clearly visible bell tower behind. No modern skyscrapers, neon or cars. Wide 16:9, ideally 2560×1440 or larger. Keep the top-left area quiet for location text and the lower quarter quiet for action controls.
2. **Drowned Bell Tower:** exterior in shallow dark water plus one belfry interior. The bell is missing. Show an empty iron yoke, hanging rope and rotten ladder. Match the square's palette and architecture. The current supplied church exterior is a temporary visual stand-in, not an exact depiction.
3. **Six matching character portraits:** Occultist, Hunter, Apothecary, Medium, Exorcist and Vagabond. Adult, restrained gothic horror, painterly detail, neutral dark backgrounds. At least 1024px portrait crops. Optional full figures should have transparent backgrounds and consistent scale. Until these exist, the supplied relic art serves explicitly as class insignia.
4. **Matching enemy art:** begin with the Bellkeeper and Abbey Warden. Bellkeeper: an empty sexton's cassock around a rusted frame and a rope, no smiling bell face. Warden: chained abbey guardian. Continue with Whisperling, Drowned Pilgrim, Hollow Stag, Grave Warden, Mirror Twin, Carrion Swarm, Hollow Hound and Weeping Statue. Match the character artist and lighting.

Supply original PNG/WebP files and the source pack name. Avoid buying a new pack solely for extra UI frames or generic hallways; those categories are already covered. Location-specific illustrations and a consistent cast will make the largest remaining difference.
